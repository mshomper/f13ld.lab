Model
Material and voxel prep — apply to every design in the comparison
AM library (docs/MATERIALS.md). “From design” keeps each design’s own material. Changing it re-solves on the next run.
Which connected pieces of the voxelized cell are analyzed.
Partial volume: each surface voxel carries its true solid fraction — exact solid fraction, stiffness grid-converged by 64³ on thin walls and struts. 0/1 cube reproduces results from before v0.19.0. Crush and buckling always use the 0/1 cube.
Your settings are remembered in this browser.
Elastic
Linear homogenization — full Cᵢⱼ, E*, ν*, directional stiffness
64³ suits most designs; 128³ for thin walls and struts (slower, more GPU memory). Thermal κ solves on the same grid.
Buckling
Linear eigenvalue buckling, free-sided (uniaxial-stress) loading
Most sparse designs need 32³ or 64³ to buckle meaningfully. Floating islands are always removed.
Crush
Nonlinear J2 plasticity with Voce hardening, adaptive load steps on the GPU
Crush always uses the 0/1 cube voxels.
The adaptive crush stops at this strain.
All crushes X, Y and Z in turn (about 3× the time); the cards then use the weakest axis.
Thermal κ
Steady-state conduction, effective conductivity tensor
Each ticked filler is a separate, fast solve; switch between them with “Pores filled with” on the Thermal κ tab. No vacuum option — no real part sits in one.
